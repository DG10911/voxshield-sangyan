The problem

Voice is the new attack surface.

India's retail investors — 16+ crore Demat accounts, 70%+ from Tier-2/3 cities — are targeted by voice-based fraud. Attackers use AI voice cloning to impersonate brokers, regulators and family. Victims realise only after the money is gone, and no accessible tool detects synthetic speech in Indic, over phone audio.

Capabilities

Built for Indic telephony.

01
Codec-profiled

Behind the codec

Profiles the channel — G.711 µ/A-law, band-limit, noise — and gates the decision. Codec costs just +2.17 pts EER.

02
Indic-first

Many languages, one line

Language ID routes every call in its own tongue — Hindi to Kashmiri, Santali, Sindhi and more.

03
Multi-evidence

Many brains, one verdict

Physics · replay · environment · cross-codec · speaker · semantic↔prosody arbitrated into an explainable score.

04
Calibrated

When it isn’t sure, it says so

Explicit abstention cuts genuine-caller false alarms by ~91% at 80% coverage.

05

Who is really calling

06
Robust

Hardened and edge-ready

PGD adversarial training + distilled student for low-end devices; on-prem, no cloud egress.
