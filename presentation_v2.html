01 · The Threat

Your voice is no longer yours alone.

Consumer voice-cloning copies a speaker from seconds of public audio. The fake sounds human, it knows the customer's name, and the bank's phone channel cannot tell the difference.

◉

Cloning is cheap and instant

Modern text-to-speech clones a voice from a voicemail or a social clip. Cheap, public, multilingual. Industry data

☎

Telephony hides the evidence

An 8 kHz G.711 line smears exactly the high-frequency artifacts most detectors depend on. Studio-trained models go blind on a real call.

⧗

The damage happens fast

Voice-authorised transfers act in minutes. A verdict after the call ends is forensics, not protection. Detection must happen in the first seconds.

Detection alone loses to a replay attack: a clone played through a speaker launders the digital artifacts. It needs layers.

02 · Real-time Pipeline

From incoming audio to a verdict in 3 seconds.

Every call streams through sliding windows and a 89-dimension feature bank into six detectors, fused by a calibrated meta-brain that outputs a risk verdict with reason codes.

Phase 2 upgrades this to an interactive 3D pipeline with live inference timers, confidence meters and packet flow. The logic and the six-detector topology are the real system.
